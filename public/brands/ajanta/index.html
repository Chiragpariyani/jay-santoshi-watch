Ajanta Clocks: Buy & Repair in Chandkheda, Ahmedabad

We sell Ajanta wall clocks, double-sided station clocks, digital LED clocks and RGB digital alarm clocks. Ajanta and Orpat clocks are in many Gujarati homes, and we also repair them and replace their movements, often keeping the original dial and frame.
