About Jay Santoshi Watch

A local watch shop and watch & clock repair service in Nigam Nagar, Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, trusted by 209+ Google reviewers (4.9★).

Jay Santoshi Watch (also known as Jay Santoshi Watch Co., Jay Santoshi Watch Company, Jai Santoshi Watch) is a neighbourhood watch store and repair shop at Shop No. 1, Swapna Shilp Complex, Nigam Nagar, Near ONGC, Visat–Sabarmati Highway, Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382424.

We help people in Chandkheda and across Ahmedabad keep their watches and clocks running. We change batteries, repair watches and wall clocks, fit new straps, chains and glass, and sell wristwatches and wall clocks for daily use and gifting.

How we work

  • We check your watch in front of you
  • We explain the problem in simple words
  • We tell you the price before starting
  • We test everything before handing it back
  • We tell you honestly if a job needs a specialist or brand service centre

What customers say

  • Friendly, honest guidance: Customers mention polite, helpful staff who explain the problem and the options before doing any work.
  • Fair, affordable prices: Reviewers say prices are reasonable, and that buying here compares well with buying online.
  • Quick repairs: Battery replacement and small repairs are usually done quickly, so many customers wait at the shop while the work is done.
  • Good range of watches and wall clocks: There's a choice of wristwatches and wall clocks for daily wear, the home and gifting.
