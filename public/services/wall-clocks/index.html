Quick answer

Jay Santoshi Watch offers wall clocks at Shop No. 1, Swapna Shilp Complex, Nigam Nagar, Near ONGC, Visat–Sabarmati Highway, Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382424. Wall clocks for living rooms, bedrooms, offices and shops, plus clocks for housewarming gifts. Open daily, 10:30 AM – 9:00 PM.

A wall clock is something everyone in the home looks at every day. At our Chandkheda shop you can choose from wall clocks in different sizes, designs and colours, for the living room, bedroom, kitchen, office or shop.

Wall clocks are also a popular housewarming (vastu / gruh pravesh) and office-opening gift. Come in and we'll help you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have silent wall clocks that don't tick?

Yes. We have sweep-movement (silent) wall clocks that are good for bedrooms and study rooms.

Is a wall clock a good housewarming gift?

Yes. Wall clocks are one of the most popular housewarming and office-opening gifts in Ahmedabad. We can help you pick a design that suits the home.
