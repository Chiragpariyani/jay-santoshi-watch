Watch & clock repair services

Watch Repair

Repairs for all common brands of analog, digital and quartz watches: stopped watches, broken crowns, loose hands and more.

Watch Battery Replacement

Fast cell replacement for quartz and digital watches, usually done while you wait.

Watch Strap & Chain Replacement

New leather, silicone and metal straps, and resizing of metal chains by adding or removing links.

Watch Glass Replacement

Replacement of cracked or scratched watch glass (crystal) for round and many shaped watches.

Watch Servicing & Cleaning

Cleaning, check-up and care for watches that run slow, fast, or haven't been looked after for a long time.

Wall Clock & Clock Repair

Repairs for quartz wall clocks, table clocks and alarm clocks, including movement (machine) replacement.
