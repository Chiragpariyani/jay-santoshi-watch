How often should a watch battery be changed?

A typical quartz watch battery lasts about 1.5 to 3 years, depending on the watch and its features. Watches with lights, alarms or chronographs can use batteries faster.

Should I remove a dead battery from my watch?

Yes. Leaving a dead battery inside for a long time can leak and damage the movement. Get it replaced soon after the watch stops.

Can you replace the battery in a water-resistant watch?

Yes. We check the gasket and close the back carefully. Water resistance can't be fully guaranteed after any opening, so avoid swimming with an older watch.
